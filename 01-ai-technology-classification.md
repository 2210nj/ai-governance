# AI Technology Classification & Governance Assessment

## Objective

Classify different AI-enabled business use cases and identify
their primary governance considerations.

## Use Cases

| Use Case | Technology | Key Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Facial recognition | Deep Learning | Privacy / Bias |
| Recommendation engine | ML | Bias / Personalization |
| Customer chatbot | Generative AI | Hallucination |
| Rules-based loan system | Rule-based AI | Fairness / Transparency |
| Fraud detection | ML | False positives / Bias |
