## My Approach to AI Governance

I view AI Governance as more than a policy or compliance exercise. Effective governance requires a clear connection between identified risks, defined responsibilities, implemented safeguards, and verifiable evidence.

My approach is guided by five fundamental questions:

### 1. What are we governing?

Identify the AI system, its intended purpose, stakeholders, users, and deployment context.

### 2. What could go wrong?

Assess potential risks to individuals, organizations, and society, considering the system's capabilities and context of use.

### 3. What controls are needed?

Translate identified risks and applicable requirements into specific, proportionate, and actionable safeguards.

### 4. How do we verify implementation?

Define control owners, implementation expectations, supporting evidence, testing approaches, and monitoring criteria.

### 5. How do we maintain accountability?

Establish governance responsibilities, human oversight, escalation mechanisms, and lifecycle review processes.

My focus is on making governance **understandable, actionable, and measurable** for the teams responsible for developing, deploying, and operating AI systems.
